About the event

VentureVerse is a student-focused event about turning early ideas into ventures. Hear how real startups began, try a hands-on idea sprint, and meet other people on campus who want to build something.

Learn from founders

Listen to honest startup journeys, including the mistakes and the turning points.

Test your own idea

Join the Campus Idea Sprint and shape a rough idea into a simple pitch in one session.

Meet your team

Network with students, mentors and EDC members who can help you take the next step.
